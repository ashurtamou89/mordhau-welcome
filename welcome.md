### HOW TO PLAY - $1/Skill Point
----------------------------
***Buy Menu Keybind (`B`):*** Purchase skills, weapons, and vote on server settings.

***Buy Skill Points:*** Type `!buy` followed by the amount. Example: `!buy 100` *Suggest Buying No More Than 240 At Once*

***Gamble:*** Type `!gamble` followed by the amount. Example: `!gamble 1000` *(Max: 999999)*

***Server Voting:*** Use the Buy Menu (`B`) to vote on difficulty, Horde AI count, 30/40/50 waves, and other server settings.

### TIPS FOR NEW PLAYERS
---------------------------
* Set up **ALL** skills right when you join.

* Don't go over **255 skill points** or it resets to zero.

* Refunding skills may reset your skill points to zero. If this happens, simply buy more.

* Spawning more than **150 Horde enemies** may cause lag spikes.
