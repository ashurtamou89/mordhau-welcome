## How To Play & What To Know

**Buy Menu Keybind (`B`):** Purchase skills, weapons, and vote on server settings.

**Buy Skill Points:** Type `!buy` followed by the amount. Example: `!buy 100`

**Gamble:** Type `!gamble` followed by the amount. Example: `!gamble 1000` *(Max: 999999)*

**Server Voting:** Use the Buy Menu (`B`) to vote on difficulty, Horde AI count, 30/40/50 waves, and other server settings.

## Tips for New Players

**1.** Set up **ALL** skills right when you join.

**2.** Don't go over **255 skill points** or it resets to zero.

**3.** Refunding skills may reset your skill points to zero. If this happens, simply buy more.

**4.** Spawning more than **150 Horde enemies** may cause lag spikes.
